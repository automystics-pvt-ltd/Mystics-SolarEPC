Build a comprehensive, enterprise-grade ERP system with a modular, scalable architecture and complete end-to-end business workflows. The ERP should include role-based access control, configurable approval workflows, audit trails, dashboards, notifications, reports, document management, and complete traceability across all modules.

### Core ERP Modules

* User & Role Management
* Vendor & Customer Management
* Material & Inventory Master
* Project Management
* Procurement Management
* Quotation Management
* Purchase Order (PO)
* Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
* GRN Return
* Invoice Management
* Stock & Warehouse Management
* Finance & Payments
* Reports & Analytics
* Dashboard & Notifications
* Audit Logs & Document Management

### Procurement Workflow

Client Request → RFQ → Vendor Quotations → Quotation Comparison (L1/L2/L3 Analysis) → Approval Workflow → Purchase Order (PO) → Vendor Acceptance → Dispatch → Delivery Tracking → Goods Receipt Note (GRN) → Quality Inspection → Stock Update → Invoice Verification (3-Way Matching: PO + GRN + Invoice) → Payment Approval → Payment → Procurement Closure.

### Quotation Management

* Create or upload quotations.
* Vendor and material creation from quotation screen.
* Multiple vendors per RFQ.
* L1/L2/L3 quotation comparison.
* Multi-level approval workflow.
* Version control.
* Unique quotation reference numbers.
* Complete audit history.
* Approval comments and revision requests.
* Auto conversion to PO after approval.

### Purchase Order (PO)

* Auto-generate PO from approved quotation.
* Unique PO numbering.
* Partial delivery support.
* Delivery schedule and milestone tracking.
* Vendor acknowledgement.
* Amendments with version history.
* Delay notifications and escalations.
* Complete approval workflow.

### Goods Receipt Note (GRN)

* Full and partial GRNs.
* Multiple deliveries against one PO.
* Quantity & quality verification.
* Damaged, excess, and shortage handling.
* Batch/Lot/Serial number tracking.
* Attachment support.
* Digital signatures.
* Approval workflow.
* Automatic stock update after GRN approval.

### GRN Return

* Return damaged or rejected materials.
* Partial or complete returns.
* Return approval workflow.
* Vendor replacement tracking.
* Credit/Debit note support.
* Automatic inventory adjustments.
* Complete return history.

### Invoice Management

* Tax/GST calculations.
* Invoice approval workflow.
* Payment status tracking.

* Vendor ledger integration.
